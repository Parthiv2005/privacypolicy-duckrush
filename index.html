01

Overview & Effective Date

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Welcome to Duck Rush, a mobile arcade game developed and published by Duck Rush Studio (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the limited data processed when you play Duck Rush on Android devices.

You can play the core Duck Rush experience offline without creating an account or sharing personal details. Data is only processed when you choose to connect Google Play Games Services (for cloud saves, achievements, and leaderboards), choose to watch a voluntary Rewarded Video Ad (to revive your duck or skip a mission), or when standard anonymous crash diagnostics are generated to keep the game running smoothly.

02

Information Collected via Google Play Games Login

Duck Rush integrates Google Play Games Services to offer optional social and cloud-sync features. Signing in with Google Play Games is completely optional—if you decline or sign out, you can still play Duck Rush locally on your device.

Data Accessed When You Sign In

  • Gamer Tag (Display Name) & Avatar: Your public Play Games nickname and profile icon, shown in-game and on public or friends leaderboards.
  • Play Games Player ID: A unique platform identifier generated by Google Play Games to associate your game save with your Play Games profile (we do not receive your Gmail password).
  • Cloud Save Progress: Stage completion, unlocked duck characters/items, mission milestones, and currency balances stored via Google Play Saved Games.
  • Achievements & Leaderboard Scores: Unlocked trophies and high-score records submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards.

Purpose of Processing: We use this information solely to sync your game progress across Android devices, display your ranking on leaderboards, and track unlocked achievements.

Google Play Games Services is governed by Google’s own privacy practices. You can review the official{" "} Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) {" "}for further details.

03

Rewarded Advertisements (Revives & Mission Skips)

Duck Rush includes voluntary, opt-in Rewarded Video Advertisements powered by Google AdMob. These ads only play when you explicitly tap a reward prompt in the game to:

1. Revive Your Duck

Continue your current run immediately after crashing into an obstacle instead of restarting from the beginning.

2. Skip Challenging Missions

Bypass a tough mission objective and advance smoothly to the next challenge when you choose to watch a video.

Data Processed by Advertising Partners

When you request or watch a rewarded advertisement, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process the following technical and advertising signals:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID): A user-resettable device identifier used to serve ads, cap ad frequency, and measure ad performance.
  • IP Address: Used to infer coarse geographic location (such as country or city) for regional compliance and to detect and block invalid traffic or ad fraud.
  • Device & App Diagnostics: Device manufacturer/model, Android OS version, screen dimensions, language settings, and basic ad interaction timestamps.
  • Ad-Completion Verification Signals: Technical callback signals confirming that you finished watching the rewarded video so Duck Rush can securely grant your in-game revive or mission skip.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads or Reset Your Advertising ID

You have full control over advertising identifiers on your Android device. At any time, open your device’s Settings → Google → All services (or Privacy) → Ads, where you can tap Reset advertising ID, Delete advertising ID, or opt out of Ads Personalization. If you delete or reset your AAID, you can still watch rewarded ads for revives and mission skips using non-personalized contextual ads.

04

Gameplay Data & Crash Diagnostics

Local Device Storage: Duck Rush stores your active mission progress, unlocked ducks, high scores, and audio/control preferences locally on your device’s internal app storage so you can resume playing instantaneously—even without an internet connection.

Anonymous Crash & Performance Metrics: To diagnose bugs, prevent freezes, and optimize frame rates across different Android phones and tablets, Duck Rush receives aggregated, anonymous crash logs, Application Not Responding (ANR) traces, and performance metrics through Google Play / Android Vitals. These diagnostics do not identify you personally.

06

Data Retention, Security & Account Deletion

Encryption in Transit: All network communications between Duck Rush, Google Play Games Services, and Google AdMob are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS protocols.

Data Retention: Local game saves remain stored on your Android device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall Duck Rush. Cloud saves and leaderboard entries tied to your Google Play Games account remain stored in Google Play Games Services until you delete them or disconnect the game.

How to Disconnect Google Play Games & Delete Your Data

In compliance with Google Play Data Deletion requirements, GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you can delete your Duck Rush data at any time using the steps below:

  1. Clear Local Device Data: Open your Android device’s Settings → Apps → Duck Rush → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the app.
  2. Delete Google Play Games Cloud Data & Revoke Access: Open the Google Play Games app on your device, tap More (⋮) → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, locate Duck Rush, and tap Delete. You may also revoke access anytime via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.
  3. Request Direct Deletion Assistance: Email us at{" "} privacy@duckrushgame.com{" "} with the subject line “Duck Rush Data Deletion Request” (including your Play Games Gamer Tag if applicable). We will confirm deletion or fulfillment of your privacy rights within 30 days.
07

Children’s Privacy

Duck Rush Studio is committed to protecting the privacy of young players in accordance with applicable laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

We do not knowingly collect personal contact information directly from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country) without verifiable parental consent. Where required by regional regulations or age-gate settings, advertising requests are restricted to non-personalized, contextual ads.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has shared personal information through third-party platform services while playing Duck Rush, please contact us at{" "} privacy@duckrushgame.com{" "} so we can promptly assist with review and deletion.
